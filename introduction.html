Introduction

NUKRAX is a professional trading suite — MetaTrader 5 Expert Advisors built on Smart Money Concepts and ICT methodology, plus the community and infrastructure around them.

What NUKRAX is

NUKRAX builds institutional-grade Expert Advisors for MetaTrader 5, focused on order flow, liquidity, and structure rather than generic indicator stacking. Every system in the lineup follows the same disciplined execution pipeline, from market open through risk evaluation, before a single order is placed.

Alongside the EAs themselves, NUKRAX includes a private community, a live trading journal, and the infrastructure that keeps everything running around the clock.

Getting started

  1. 01Create an account — takes less than a minute, just an email and password.
  2. 02Confirm your email — every account is verified before it's active.
  3. 03Head to Execution Systems and pick the EA that matches your style.
  4. 04Join the Community to see what other traders are building and ask questions.

Choosing an EA

Each NUKRAX Expert Advisor is built around a specific execution style — some prioritize high-frequency precision, others are built around slower, higher-conviction SMC/ICT setups. The Execution Systems page breaks down entry models, exit logic, and risk handling for every system currently available.

APEX HFT Elite
High-frequency execution across multiple timeframes.
Aurum HFT Elite
Structured trade flow with layered risk controls.
SMC ICT Elite
Pure Smart Money Concepts and ICT entry logic.

Community
