# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

Interactions in this repository — issues, discussions, or any other public communication — are expected to remain respectful and professional.

## Not Acceptable

- Harassment, personal attacks, or discriminatory language
- Spam, unrelated self-promotion, or abusive behavior
- Impersonating NUKRAX, Cosmo Lanex, or misrepresenting affiliation with either
- Attempts to use this repository for purposes outside its intended scope

## Enforcement

Reports of unacceptable behavior can be sent to [contact@nukrax.com](mailto:contact@nukrax.com). Violations may result in comments being removed, issues being closed, or the user being blocked, at the maintainer's discretion.
