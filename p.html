This Privacy Policy explains how NUKRAX ("we", "us", or "our") handles information when you visit our website and use our services, including our Expert Advisor suite and trading platforms (nukrax.tr and nukrax.cr).

In short: we do not collect personal information from visitors to this site, and we do not use, sell, or share any personal data — because we don't have any to begin with. The sections below explain exactly what that means in practice.

01

Information We Collect

We do not collect personal information through this website. There is no account registration, and we do not collect names, email addresses, payment details, government IDs, or any other personally identifiable information as part of normal use of the site.

The only thing stored is a single local preference:

  • Cookie Consent Preference — Your choice on the cookie banner, stored only in your own browser via localStorage under the key nukrax_cookie_consent. This never leaves your device and is never transmitted to us.

If you choose to contact us directly (for example, by email or Telegram), we will naturally see whatever information you include in that message — that's between you and us, and is covered in Section 12.

02

How We Use Information

Because we do not collect personal information, there is nothing for us to use, sell, profile, or process. We do not run advertising, analytics tracking, or automated decision-making on this site.

If you reach out to us directly, we use whatever you share only to respond to that specific enquiry — nothing more.

03

Cookies & Tracking

We do not use tracking cookies, analytics cookies, or advertising cookies of any kind.

  • Essential Storage Only — The only browser storage used is your cookie consent preference, saved locally via localStorage under the key nukrax_cookie_consent, so we don't repeat the prompt unnecessarily. It is never sent to our servers or any third party.
  • No Tracking — We do not use cookies, pixels, or scripts to track your activity across this site or elsewhere.

You can clear this preference at any time by clearing your browser's localStorage data for this site, or by adjusting your browser settings. Doing so will not affect your ability to use the site.

04

Data Sharing

Since we do not collect personal information, there is nothing for us to sell, rent, trade, or share with third parties.

Any third-party services embedded in or linked from this site (e.g. YouTube, Telegram, GitHub, X) are operated independently and are governed by their own privacy policies, which we encourage you to review if you choose to interact with them.

05

Data Retention

As we do not collect personal information, there is no personal data retention period to disclose.

  • Your cookie consent preference stays in your browser until you clear it or revoke consent manually — we never receive a copy of it.
  • Any message you send us directly (email, Telegram, etc.) is kept only as long as needed to resolve your enquiry.
06

Your Rights

Because we do not collect or hold personal data about visitors to this site, there is generally nothing on file for us to grant access to, correct, or erase.

If you've contacted us directly and would like that communication reviewed or deleted, or if you believe we hold any information about you, contact us using the details in Section 12 and we'll address it directly.

07

Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your information against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. These include:

  • HTTPS encryption for all data transmitted to and from the site.
  • Restricted access to any collected data on a need-to-know basis.
  • Regular review of our data handling practices and third-party service providers.

No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your data, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Reporting a Vulnerability

If you discover a security issue affecting our website, platforms, or services, please report it privately rather than disclosing it publicly. Include a clear description of the issue, steps to reproduce it, and any relevant evidence.

Contact: security@nukrax.com

Reports are reviewed as they come in. There is no formal bug bounty program at this time, but responsible disclosure is appreciated and will be acknowledged.
