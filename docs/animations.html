Architecture

Animations

The motion system across NUKRAX — reveal-on-scroll, the hero cube, and live data animation.

The motion system

Animation on NUKRAX is used to reinforce hierarchy and give live data a sense of motion — not as decoration for its own sake. Every animated pattern respects prefers-reduced-motion.

Reveal-on-scroll

Headings and cards animate in as they enter the viewport, using IntersectionObserver rather than a scroll listener — headings split into individual words that fade, rise, and un-blur with a slight stagger; cards and images fade/rise as a whole. Once a heading has revealed, it's unobserved (one-shot); decorative cards can reverse if scrolled back out of view.

Hero cube

The homepage hero features a Three.js 3×3×3 Rubik's-cube animation (assets/cube/cube.js), which checks prefers-reduced-motion before animating and pauses when the tab is backgrounded.

Live data motion

  • The homepage scroll-progress bar tracks read position across the page.
  • The equity graph on the trading terminal draws itself in on load.
  • Count-up numbers animate from zero to their target value once visible.
  • A subtle heartbeat/pulse effect marks live indicators (e.g. the Market Map).

Auto-scrolling tracks

Testimonial and timeline sections auto-scroll horizontally at a steady pace, pausing on hover/touch and resuming after a short delay. These loops cache their scrollable range rather than recomputing it every frame — see Performance.

Respecting reduced motion

Note
Every animated pattern on the site checks prefers-reduced-motion (directly, or via the shared prefersReducedMotion() helper in assets/data/device.js) and either skips the animation or reduces it to an instant state change.
