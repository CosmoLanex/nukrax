Get started

Getting Started

Create an account, find your way around the platform, and pick your first Expert Advisor.

Create your account

  1. 01Sign up with an email and password, or continue with GitHub via OAuth.
  2. 02Confirm your email — every account is verified via a confirmation link before it's fully active.
  3. 03Complete your profile — choose a username (an @handle, checked for availability in real time) and an avatar.
  4. 04You're in. You'll land on your account dashboard, ready to explore.

Finding your way around

Once you're signed in, the navigation bar adapts to show account-specific actions instead of the signed-out "Get Started" prompt. The main areas you'll use:

AreaWhat it's for
Execution SystemsCompare and choose between the three Expert Advisors.
CommunityA private feed for NUKRAX traders — posts, likes, follows, and notifications.
AccountYour dashboard: profile, order history, and quick links.
ProfileYour public-facing profile page, visible to other community members.
SettingsAccount preferences, security, and notification controls.

Picking your first EA

If you're not sure where to start, head to Execution Systems — it lays out each EA's execution style side by side. For a deeper breakdown of how to choose, see Quick Start and Execution Systems.

Tip
Not sure which system fits your style? The Community is a good place to ask — other traders are usually happy to share what's worked for them.
